Spike sorting

Parameters (SAMS defaults) and method
Per electrode, following the SAMS pipeline (Ren et al. 2025): PCA of the waveforms → clusters on PC1–PC2, number chosen by the Davies–Bouldin index (k-means; SAMS uses spectral clustering) → waveform outliers removed → clusters that Hartigan's dip test finds multimodal on PC1 or PC2 are split → clusters with similar mean waveforms (dynamic time warping) are merged → final outlier and refractory-period clean-up, slow units dropped. Removed spikes are “unsorted”. Results are deterministic for a given file and parameters.
